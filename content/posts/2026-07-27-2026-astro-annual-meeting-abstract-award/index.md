Happy to share more good news: our work has now also been selected to receive a **2026 ASTRO Annual Meeting Abstract Award** — the **Basic Transitional Award in Physics** — one of only **30 award-winning abstracts** chosen from more than **2,000 submissions**.

**Abstract title:** *Early Adaptive Interventions in Lung Cancer: Leveraging Fusion of Longitudinal CBCT Trajectories and Clinical Variables for Robust Survival Prediction*

This is the same work we [previously shared](/posts/2026-05-18-astro-2026-best-of-physics-oral-acceptance/) was selected for an **Oral Scientific Presentation** in the *BEST of Physics* session — so this abstract is now recognized both for its oral presentation slot and with an Annual Meeting Abstract Award. Full presentation and author details are in that earlier post.

Grateful to the ASTRO Annual Meeting Scientific Program Committee, and to every collaborator who contributed to this project. Looking forward to presenting in Boston this September.
