Our group had **four abstracts** at the **ASTRO 2026 Annual Meeting** in Boston: three oral presentations, one of them in the *BEST of Physics* session, and one poster. I am the principal investigator on the first abstract and a collaborator on the other three.

## Abstracts

| Title | Presenter | Format | Date and time (ET) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early Adaptive Interventions in Lung Cancer: Leveraging Fusion of Longitudinal CBCT Trajectories and Clinical Variables for Robust Survival Prediction | Wookjin Choi, PhD | Oral, *BEST of Physics* | Sep 28, 11:05-11:15 |
| Cross-Institutional Validation of LLM-Based Cardiac Event Extraction from Electronic Health Records | Wenchao Cao, PhD | Oral | Sep 28, 08:40-08:50 |
| Integrating Clinical and PET Radiomics Features for Pre-Therapy Cardiac Risk Prediction | Devanshu Panchal, PhD | Quick Pitch Oral | Sep 30, 09:15-09:20 |
| How Many Plans Are Enough? Defining Minimum Cohort Size for Stable Population-Based Plan Quality Scoring | Cella Kove, BA | Poster | Sep 28, 10:45-12:00 |

The first abstract was announced earlier: see [Selected for ASTRO 2026 BEST of Physics](/posts/2026-05-18-astro-2026-best-of-physics-oral-acceptance/) and the [Basic Transitional Award in Physics](/posts/2026-07-27-2026-astro-annual-meeting-abstract-award/) follow-up.

## Themes

The abstracts cover four areas: longitudinal CBCT imaging for lung cancer, LLM-based information extraction from electronic health records, cardiac risk prediction with PET radiomics, and quality scoring for radiotherapy plans. The cardiac event extraction work builds on [TRACER](/posts/2026-07-01-tracer-open-source-llms-cardiac-event-extraction/).

Thank you to the presenters and to every collaborator who contributed to this work.
