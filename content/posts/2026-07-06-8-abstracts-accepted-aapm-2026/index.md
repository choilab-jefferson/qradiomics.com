Proud to share that our research group has **8 abstracts accepted** for presentation at the **AAPM 2026 Annual Meeting** in Vancouver! It's been a privilege to work with such an outstanding team and collaborators across a broad range of projects — AI-based auto-segmentation, LLM-driven pipelines, radiomics-based toxicity and outcome prediction, and automated clinical QA platforms.

I won't be attending the meeting in Vancouver in person this year, but I'm incredibly proud of the team members and collaborators presenting this work. If you're at AAPM 2026, please stop by and say hello! Aside from one SNAP Oral presentation, all of our accepted abstracts will be presented as posters this year.

## Accepted abstracts

1. **PO-DPV-R-6** — *Auto-Segmentation of Metastatic Liver Tumors in SIRT*
   Jun Li, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy, **Wookjin Choi**

2. **PO-DPV-T-272** — *Novel Constraint-Based Method to Evaluate and Score Patient Radiotherapy Plans*
   Devanshu Panchal, Vikas Aragam, Cella Maria Kove, Sonia Marhefka, David H. Thomas, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy, **Wookjin Choi**

3. **SU-115-220-8** — *Multi-Tier Automated Pipeline for TG-263 Structure Name Standardization Using Rule-Based, NLP, and RAG-Enhanced LLM Matching*
   **Wookjin Choi**, Hamidreza Nourzadeh, David H. Thomas, Yingxuan Chen, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy

4. **SU-315-GPD-R-23** — *Improving Cardiac Toxicity Prediction from PET Radiomics: Cross-Cohort Validation and Late-Fusion Ensemble Modeling*
   Wenchao Cao, Devanshu Panchal, Nilanjan Halder, Michael Dichmann, Patrick Faherty, Erik Blomain, **Wookjin Choi**, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy

5. **SU-315-GPD-T-25** — *Hierarchical Spatial and Temporal Aggregation as Progressive Denoising for Longitudinal CBCT Radiomics in Lung Cancer* — **SNAP Oral**
   **Wookjin Choi**, Pradeep Bhetwal, Michael Dichmann, Yingcui Jia, Wenchao Cao, Yingxuan Chen, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy, Hamidreza Nourzadeh

6. **SU-315-GPD-T-141** — *Automated MPPG5b-Compliant Platform for Photon Beam Model Validation Across an Integrated Health Care System*
   Hamidreza Nourzadeh, David H. Thomas, Vimal K. Desai, Wenchao Cao, **Wookjin Choi**

7. **SU-315-GPD-T-630** — *Novel LLM-Based Cardiotoxicity Extraction from Electronic Health Records*
   Wenchao Cao, **Wookjin Choi**, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy

8. **SU-315-GPD-T-1025** — *A Vendor-Agnostic Radiation Treatment Plan Quality Assurance Software Implemented for an Integrated Health System*
   Hamidreza Nourzadeh, **Wookjin Choi**, Karl W. Mund, Jun Kang, Lei Fu, Firas Mourtada, Adam Dicker, Yevgeniy Vinogradskiy

*Department of Radiation Oncology, Sidney Kimmel Medical College, Thomas Jefferson University, Philadelphia, PA (with Thomas Jefferson University Hospital and Drexel University College of Medicine co-authors).*
